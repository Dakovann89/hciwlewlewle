Rewards & Loyalty Program

Every meal brings you closer to something special.
Join our loyalty program and enjoy exclusive rewards, birthday surprises, and seasonal perks
— because our most loyal guests deserve to be celebrated.

Loyalty Rewards

Benvenuto Bonus (Welcome Reward)
Join Mamma Mia's loyalty program and instantly receive 100 bonus points to kickstart your rewards journey.

Punti Fedeltà (Loyalty Points)
Earn 1 point for every $1 spent. Accumulate points and redeem them for free dishes, discounts, and exclusive perks.

Birthday Rewards

Compleanno Speciale (Birthday Special)
Celebrate your birthday with a complimentary slice of Classic Tiramisu, on us. Valid throughout your birthday month.

Birthday Discount
Enjoy 20% off your entire order during your birthday week — dine in or order online.

Milestone Rewards

Prima Visita (First Visit)
Get a free Insalata Caprese on your first dine-in visit to Mamma Mia Ristorante.

10th Visit Treat
Reach your 10th visit and enjoy a complimentary Gelato of your choice as a thank-you from our kitchen.

Seasonal Rewards

Festival della Pasta
Every September, loyalty members get 15% off all pasta dishes in celebration of Italian Food Month.

Holiday Gift Card Bonus
Purchase a gift card during the holiday season and receive 10% extra credit added to the card value.

How It Works

  • Sign Up — Create your free Mamma Mia account online or at the restaurant.
  • Earn Points — Get 1 point for every $1 spent on every order.
  • Redeem Rewards — Use your points to unlock free dishes, discounts, and exclusive perks.
  • Level Up — The more you visit, the better your rewards get.

Terms & Conditions

  • Points are non-transferable and cannot be exchanged for cash.
  • Rewards must be redeemed within 12 months of earning.
  • Birthday rewards are valid for the birthday month only.
  • Mamma Mia Ristorante reserves the right to modify the program at any time.
